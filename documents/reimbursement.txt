
EMPLOYEE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Travel Expenses

Employees travelling for approved business purposes may claim eligible travel expenses.
Business travel must be approved by the reporting manager before booking.
Employees must submit valid receipts for reimbursable travel expenses.

Accommodation

Hotel accommodation expenses incurred during approved business travel may be reimbursed.
Employees should select reasonably priced accommodation.
Premium accommodation requires additional management approval.

Food Expenses

Employees may claim eligible meal expenses during approved business travel.
Meal claims must be supported by valid receipts.
Personal meals outside approved business travel are not reimbursable.

Reimbursement Submission

Employees must submit reimbursement requests through the company expense portal.
Each request must include the expense date, amount, business purpose and supporting receipts.
Incomplete reimbursement requests may be returned for correction.

Approval Process

The reporting manager reviews submitted reimbursement requests.
Finance verifies approved claims before processing payment.
Reimbursement processing timelines depend on internal finance procedures.

Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Personal shopping, entertainment expenses and expenses unrelated to approved business activities are not reimbursable.
