
INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

Password Security

Employees must use strong and unique passwords for company accounts.
Passwords must not be shared with colleagues or external individuals.
Employees should enable multi-factor authentication wherever supported.
Suspected password compromise must be reported immediately.

Access Control

Employees receive access to systems according to their job responsibilities.
Access to confidential information must be restricted to authorized personnel.
Employees must not attempt to access systems or data without permission.

Data Protection

Confidential company information must be stored only in approved company systems.
Employees must not transfer confidential documents to personal email accounts.
Sensitive information must not be shared with unauthorized third parties.

Device Security

Employees must keep company devices updated with approved security patches.
Devices must be locked when left unattended.
Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to the IT department immediately.

Security Incidents

Employees must report suspected phishing emails, malware infections and unauthorized access.
Security incidents should be reported to the IT security team as soon as possible.
Employees must not investigate or modify potentially compromised systems without authorization.
