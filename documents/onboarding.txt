
EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING POLICY

Before Joining

New employees must submit the required employment documents before their joining date.
These documents may include identity proof, educational certificates and previous employment records.
The HR department verifies submitted documents during onboarding.

First Day

Employees receive their company identification credentials during the joining process.
The HR team provides an introduction to company policies and workplace procedures.
New employees are introduced to their reporting manager and assigned team.

IT Setup

The IT department provides access to approved company systems.
Employees receive their official company email account.
Required software and development tools are installed according to job responsibilities.

Training

New employees must complete mandatory orientation and security awareness training.
Department-specific training is coordinated by the reporting manager.
Employees should complete assigned onboarding activities within the communicated timelines.

Probation

New employees may be subject to a probation period as specified in their employment agreement.
Performance and progress are reviewed by the reporting manager.
Probation confirmation is communicated through the HR department.
