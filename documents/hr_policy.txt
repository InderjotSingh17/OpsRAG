
HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY

Working Hours

The standard working schedule is Monday to Friday.
Employees are expected to work eight hours per day.
The standard office working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Employees receive a one-hour lunch break.

Attendance

Employees must record their attendance using the company's attendance system.
Employees arriving more than 15 minutes after their scheduled start time are marked late.
Repeated late arrivals may result in a discussion with the reporting manager.

Remote Work

Employees may request remote work approval from their reporting manager.
Remote work arrangements must be approved before the employee works remotely.
Employees working remotely must remain available during standard working hours.

Public Holidays

The company observes officially announced public holidays.
The annual holiday calendar is shared with employees at the beginning of each year.

Overtime

Employees must obtain prior approval from their reporting manager before working overtime.
Overtime eligibility and compensation are determined according to company policy.
